OUR IDEA

Less buying for one-time needs.

NearRent helps neighbours share useful products through clear rental terms and documented handovers.

01

Local by design

Explore listings around your selected neighbourhood. The prototype is designed around a 10-kilometre search radius when location coordinates are provided.

02

Transparent by default

See the rental fee, deposit, delivery contribution and proposed refund before you make arrangements.

03

Evidence, not guesswork

Booking-linked messages, condition reports and return confirmations help both sides keep a clear record.

A note about safety

NearRent helps record agreements and reports; it cannot guarantee that fraud or damage will never happen. Meet safely, protect personal information, and follow the agreed rental terms.
