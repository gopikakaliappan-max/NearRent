Terms & conditions

Starter policy text for this project demo. Review and adapt it before any real-world launch.

1. Platform role

NearRent is designed to connect item owners and renters and record booking details. A production service needs professionally reviewed terms that clearly define each party's legal obligations.

2. Accurate listings

Owners should provide accurate descriptions, rental prices, replacement values, existing-condition notes and availability. Do not list prohibited, unsafe or unlawful items.

3. Rental and delivery agreement

Both parties should agree to rental dates, pickup or owner-arranged delivery, delivery-cost split, return arrangements, late-return rules and any security deposit before handover.

4. Condition and damage

Photographs and written condition reports are evidence, not automatic proof of fault. Any proposed deduction should be explained, proportionate, and handled through a fair review process if disputed.

5. Privacy and messaging

Use only necessary personal information. Do not post private phone numbers, home addresses, identity documents or payment details publicly. Booking messages should only be accessible to the booking's renter and owner, and authorised administrators where necessary.

6. Safety and disputes

Meet in a safe place, inspect the item together and report suspicious activity. The demo does not guarantee user identity, product authenticity, payment, delivery or a fraud-free transaction.

7. Production readiness

This student project is not a payment processor, identity-verification provider or legal service. Real payments, personal-data processing, consumer terms and dispute handling need further security, legal and operational review.
